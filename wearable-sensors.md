# Wearable health sensors

- PPG (photoplethysmography): optical sensor for heart rate and SpO2, sensitive to motion artefacts
- Accelerometer/IMU: activity tracking, step counting and fall detection
- ECG patches and smartwatches: single-lead recordings, useful for detecting atrial fibrillation
- Continuous glucose monitors: measure glucose in interstitial fluid with a small electrochemical sensor

Main challenges: motion artefacts, battery life, data privacy and clinical validation.
