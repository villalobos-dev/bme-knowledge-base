# Medical imaging

## X-ray and CT
- Use ionising radiation; tissues are distinguished by how much they attenuate X-rays
- CT reconstructs slices from many projections; values are expressed in Hounsfield units (water = 0, air = -1000)

## MRI
- Uses a strong magnetic field and radio-frequency pulses on hydrogen nuclei, with no ionising radiation
- T1 and T2 weighting give different tissue contrast

## Ultrasound
- Sends 2-15 MHz sound waves and builds an image from the echoes
- Real-time, portable and radiation-free; Doppler mode measures blood flow
